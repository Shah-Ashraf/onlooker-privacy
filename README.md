# Onlooker — privacy policy

This repository exists for one reason: to serve the privacy policy for the Android app
**Onlooker** at a stable public address, which Google Play requires of any app that handles
camera and biometric data.

**Published at:** https://shah-ashraf.github.io/onlooker-privacy/

`index.html` is the whole of it. The app's own source lives in a separate, private repository.

## Keeping it in sync

The policy is written and maintained in the app repository as `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`, which is
the source of truth. `index.html` here is the rendered copy. When the policy changes:

1. edit `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` in the app repository,
2. regenerate `docs/privacy-policy.html` there,
3. copy it here as `index.html` and push.

A policy that disagrees with itself across copies is worse than one that is merely out of
date, so all three move together or none of them do.
